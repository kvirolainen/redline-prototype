# Dealer research

Who should be the first Redline dealer, and can public data find them?

**Short answer:** public data shows *what kind* of dealer to call first, not *which one*. Who sends how many cars to auction is visible only to dealers inside EBlock and OPENLANE.

## What public data shows

- **Auction is a weak exit.** In a given week only about 45–70% of cars in Canadian auction lanes sell ([CBB weekly, 2026](https://www.canadianblackbook.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/CBB_MarketInsights_06302026.pdf)). The rest are re-run, with more fees and transport.
- **Each sale costs money and time.** US auction charges average about $385 a car, plus $100–200 transport. No public Canadian all-in figure exists; **C$400–800 a car** is a placeholder to check with a dealer.
- **It's a few cars per store.** Roughly **5–10 a month** per dealership go to auction. Enough to test price and process with one dealer; scale means many dealers.

## Who to call first

| Dealer type | Fit | First deal |
|---|---|---|
| **Luxury store, owner-run** | ✅ Off-brand trade-ins and own-brand cars past the certified pre-owned limit: 3–9 years, mainstream, Clutch's range | ✅ One decision-maker; Clutch doesn't compete with their certified stock |
| **EV-only brands** (Rivian, Lucid) | ✅ Nearly every trade-in; no used lot in Canada | ⚠️ Corporate deal, but Tesla is the template |
| **Mainstream import store** | ⚠️ More old, high-km cars | ⚠️ More cherry-picking worry |
| **Big groups** | Same cars | ❌ Already own an exit (AutoCanada owns North Toronto Auction) |
| **Independent lots** | Few | ❌ They buy at auction more than they sell |

**Pick: one owner-run luxury store.** Clutch's wholesale desk already sells the cars Clutch doesn't keep, so Redline can take a dealer's whole batch. That answers the cherry-picking worry.

## How we'd make the list real

1. **Start with Clutch's own data.** The Dealer & Wholesale team works these auctions every week: rank the GTA dealers it sees selling the most.
2. **Call 10 used-car managers** with three questions: trade-ins to auction a month, net per car after fees, days until paid.
3. **Price against Canadian Black Book wholesale by VIN**, so Back office's "above auction" is a real number.
4. **Agree on the scorecard before the first deal:** dollars above auction, re-price rate, days to cash. Back office's Overview already tracks them.
