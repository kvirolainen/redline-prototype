# Demo

Press **Reset demo** in the header first. It's **Thu Nov 12, 10:40**: pickup day at Yorkdale Honda, and Sam, the inspector, is on the lot. Switch modules from the top-right switcher.

## Showcase: one car, one re-price

1. **Trade-in.** Demo cars → **2020 Honda HR-V**. Answer the questions in any order; the price appears once all are in. **Accept offer**: it joins the Nov 19 pickup.
2. **Clutch inspector.** Open the **Civic** → take the VIN and odometer photos → open one area → **Different** → add a finding and photo → **Send re-price to Dave**.
3. **Clutch ops.** "On the lot now": the Civic is waiting on Dave, with the time Sam has to leave.
4. **Back office.** The re-price leads **Needs you** → **Accept**.
5. **Clutch inspector.** The Civic → **Loaded on the truck**.
6. **Skip to next pickup day** in the header: the Civic is paid and the HR-V is on Sam's next run.

## Explore more

- **Trade-in:** the **2013 F-150** (over 200,000 km) goes to a Clutch appraiser; its offer lands in Back office about 45 seconds later.
- **Back office:** **Keep** a car for the dealer's own lot, tick **Ready**, accept the appraiser-priced **2012 Camry**, open **Results**.
- **Clutch ops:** expand **Scarborough** (high re-price rate) and **Downtown Toyota** (a car arrives after its run); open **Planning**.
- **Clutch inspector:** mark the **CX-5** **Not ready**; confirm Vaughan Ford's **F-150** with **All match**; in step 4, **Decline** instead and the car stays with the dealer.
