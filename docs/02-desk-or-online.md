# Desk or online?

Where does the trade-in happen: **at the dealer's desk** or **online, before the visit**?

**The main trade-off:** the desk makes dealers easier to sign up. Online is easier for Clutch to run.

## A. At the desk: easier for dealers

The sales manager does the trade-in in Redline instead of haggling.

- ✅ **Nothing changes for the dealer** except the haggling. That's an easy yes for a first pilot.
- ⚠️ **New work for Clutch:** a dealer-facing flow, and a screen that serves the customer and the dealer at once.

## B. Online: easier for Clutch

The customer does Clutch's existing sell flow at home and brings the offer to a participating dealer.

- ✅ **Reuses what Clutch already has:** the same flow, pricing and inspection. Tesla and Kelley Blue Book's Instant Cash Offer already work this way.
- ✅ **The customer still has a reason to go through the dealer:** in Ontario, a trade-in at the dealer lowers the HST on the new car.
- ⚠️ **Harder to sign dealers:** the customer meets Clutch first, and the price is set outside the store.

## What this prototype does

**The desk first, to win the first dealer.** The offer is stored as a record with an expiry, so an online quote can plug into the same pickup, inspection and payment later.
